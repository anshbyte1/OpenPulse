# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Expected behavior includes being considerate, giving constructive feedback, respecting differing viewpoints, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information without permission.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
